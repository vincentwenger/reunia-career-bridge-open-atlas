Responsible interview preparation

Practice support without hidden real-interview assistance

Standard Career Bridge accounts focus on preparation, mock interviews, and post-practice coaching. Restricted Live Interview Assistance can be enabled for administrators and approved groups.

  • Practice-only recording — record mock sessions you deliberately start.
  • Selected evidence — choose the documents and examples used for coaching.
  • No desktop client in the MVP — the browser recorder is the supported path.
  • Separate administration — operational analytics and incidents remain administrator-only.

Frequently asked questions

What to know before your first application

These answers describe the current Career Bridge MVP.

Still have a question? Contact support →
What is an Application Workspace?+

It is the job-specific workspace connecting the target job, resume, materials, evidence, interview preparation, practice sessions, actions, and status.

Can I reuse my background and evidence?+

Yes. Career Profile and Career Evidence Library are reusable, while each application selects only the context relevant to that target role.

Do I need to install a recorder?+

No. Mock Interview Recorder runs in the browser. The Windows Desktop Recorder is excluded from the Career Bridge MVP.

Does Réunia answer questions during a real interview?+

No. The product supports preparation, mock interviews, and review. Candidate-facing real-time answer generation is retired.

What does the Interview Scorecard measure?+

It evaluates answer relevance, evidence, structure, clarity, and delivery.

Where do improvement tasks go?+

Career Action Plan tracks resume changes, practice needs, applications, and recruiter follow-ups.
