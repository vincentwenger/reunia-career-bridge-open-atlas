Responsible meeting intelligence

Control what is recorded and what context is provided

Réunia is designed around deliberate user actions. Start and stop recordings yourself, select the documents that matter, and keep temporary meeting materials separate from your reusable library.

  • User-controlled recording — recording begins only after you start it.
  • Selected AI context — choose the permanent or temporary documents used for a meeting.
  • Responsible use reminder — inform participants and obtain any consent required before recording.
  • Clear support path — contact support with data handling or deletion questions.

Frequently asked questions

What to know before your first meeting

These answers explain the current product direction without making unsupported security, compatibility, or retention promises.

Still have a question? Contact support →
Do I need to install an application?+

No installation is required for the browser meeting recorder. The Windows desktop recorder is optional for users who need additional recording controls.

Can I upload my own documents?+

Yes. You can maintain a reusable document library, select library files for a meeting, or add temporary meeting-specific materials.

What does Live Q&A do?+

Live Q&A provides contextual AI assistance during a meeting using the guidance and documents selected for that meeting.

What can I review after a meeting?+

The review workspace can organize the transcript, summary, questions, decisions, scorecard, insights, and follow-up actions.

Can it help with interviews?+

Yes. You can add the role, company, job description, and preparation documents, then review answer relevance and communication metrics afterward.

Do I need participant consent before recording?+

You should inform participants and obtain any consent required by applicable law, company policy, or meeting rules before recording.

How is meeting data handled?+

Specific storage, retention, deletion, and security commitments should be confirmed in the deployed product documentation. Contact support for the current implementation details.
