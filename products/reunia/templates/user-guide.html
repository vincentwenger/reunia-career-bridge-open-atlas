How to prepare one upcoming meeting
  1. Open Meeting Materials and create or select the upcoming meeting.
  2. Select reusable files from the Document Library and add temporary files when needed.
  3. Save the meeting materials.
  4. Open AI Context to add optional meeting-specific objectives or instructions.
  5. In the Browser Recorder, choose the prepared meeting before starting.

Browser Meeting Recorder

The recommended way to capture a meeting from your browser.

  1. 1
    Open the meeting first.Join the meeting in its browser tab or desktop application before starting the recorder.
  2. 2
    Select a prepared meeting when applicable.This links its saved materials and context to the recording. You can also choose not to link one.
  3. 3
    Choose your audio.Your microphone is recorded. Turn on Capture shared meeting audio only when you also need the other participants.
  4. 4
    Select Start recording.Allow microphone or screen-sharing permissions when the browser asks.
  5. 5
    Keep the recorder page open.You can return to the meeting tab, but do not close or refresh the recorder page.
  6. 6
    Save or discard.Select Stop & Process to create the meeting review, or Discard Recording to delete the recording.

Quick help

Common problems

Open a question for the most likely solution.

The recording will not start

Use a current desktop version of Chrome or Edge. Allow microphone access when prompted. When capturing shared meeting audio, select a tab, window, or screen that the browser can share.

The other participants are missing from the recording

Before recording, turn on Capture shared meeting audio. When the browser asks what to share, select the meeting source and enable its audio-sharing option. Microphone capture alone records only your side.

The Real-Time Meeting Feed is empty

Confirm that the recorder or connected live source is running. In Live Q&A Settings, verify that automatic answers are enabled for the source you expect: microphone, speaker, or clipboard.

My meeting is missing from Meeting Review

Confirm that you selected Stop & Process, kept the recorder page open, and waited for the saved confirmation. A discarded recording will not appear in Meeting Review.

Knowledge Search did not find the information

Check the selected search scope and filters. Confirm that the relevant document was uploaded, the meeting package was saved, or the previous meeting finished processing. Try a shorter, more specific question.

The Scorecard evaluated the wrong speaker

Open Meeting Review Settings and change Scorecard Source to your microphone, the other participant’s speaker audio, or all available audio sources.

An action is not visible

Clear the Action Center filters and check the Closed view. Open actions, completed actions, owner, meeting, due date, priority, status, and search filters can all change what is displayed.

🛡

Record and share responsibly

Inform participants and obtain any consent required by applicable laws and workplace rules. Review sensitive transcript content before creating a share link.
