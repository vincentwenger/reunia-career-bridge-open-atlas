Common questions

Quick answers

Search the current features before sending a request.

⌕
Where should I start?

Use Career Bridge to create an Application Workspace, connect Application Materials and your Career Profile, prepare evidence-backed answers, run a Mock Interview, then review the transcript, Interview Scorecard, Career Action Plan, and Career Progress.

What is included in interview preparation?

Application Materials stores the resume, job posting, company notes, and recruiter messages for one target position. Career Profile stores professional background, accomplishments, preferences, and constraints. Career Evidence Library searches verified projects, achievements, and experience.

How do I finish or discard a browser recording?

Select Stop & Process to upload, transcribe, analyze, and save the mock-interview session. Select Discard Recording to stop without creating a saved practice session.

Why is interviewer prompt audio missing?

Turn on Capture interviewer audio before starting, choose the practice prompt tab or screen, and enable the browser option to share its audio. Microphone audio is captured separately.

Does Réunia answer questions during a real interview?

No. Candidate-facing Live Q&A is retired. Réunia supports interview preparation, browser-based mock interviews, and post-practice coaching rather than hidden real-time assistance.

Where do I find mock-interview results?

Open Interview Review and select a saved practice session. Use Summary, Interview Scorecard, Transcript, or Ask about this interview. Sharing controls can create a read-only link with optional expiration, password, transcript, scorecard, and download access.

How do I find open or completed actions?

Open Career Action Plan. The default view shows open actions. Use My actions, All, or Closed and refine the list by owner, application, due date, priority, status, or search.

What does Career Progress show?

Career Progress summarizes score trends, answer-quality patterns, recurring improvement areas, completed actions, and readiness across applications and mock interviews.

Should I use Interview Review or Career Evidence Library?

Use Interview Review for one selected mock interview. Use Career Evidence Library when you need verified examples, achievements, application materials, or evidence from earlier practice sessions.

What should I include in a problem report?

Include the feature or page, what you clicked, what happened, what you expected, and the application workspace or approximate practice time when relevant. Add a screenshot only when it is safe to share.

What may I record and share?

Use recording for authorized mock-interview practice. Follow applicable consent laws and data-handling rules, and review sensitive content before sharing an Interview Review.
