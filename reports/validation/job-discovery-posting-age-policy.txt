Job Discovery source-independent posting-age policy
Validated: 2026-07-31

Implemented behavior
- DiscoverySearchPreferences.maximum_posting_age_days defaults to 30.
- The setting applies after normalization to Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, and Manual JSON-LD sources.
- Available UI choices: 7, 14, 30, 60, or Any age.
- Normalized fetched postings are retained in the shared public catalog before a user's age filter is applied.
- Unknown-date postings remain eligible.
- A future valid-through/application-closing date overrides the age limit.
- Explicit evergreen or continuous-hiring metadata overrides the age limit.
- Older postings are excluded from that user's active results before Stage 1 and before new AI assessment.
- Saved postings and postings already converted to an Application Workspace remain visible.
- Manual administrator refreshes and external scheduled scans populate the same shared catalog.
- Each user can independently narrow the centrally collected catalog with a private posting-age preference.
- Existing persisted preference records without this field load with the 30-day default.
