Job Discovery two-stage ranking validation
============================================

Implemented behavior
--------------------
1. Stage 1 evaluates every collected job without an AI call using title terms,
   locations, workplace preferences, employment type, salary bounds, excluded
   terms, explicit eligibility blockers, and deterministic skill/title overlap.
2. Jobs failing mandatory preferences remain discovery records but are exposed
   in DiscoveryResult.filtered_jobs and do not reach ResumeAI.
3. Stage 2 calls ResumeAI.analyze_job() only for Stage-1 survivors whose
   description analysis is not already stored.
4. Requirements are matched only to verified Career Profile and Evidence
   Library facts and classified as supported, partial, or unsupported.
5. The shared career_bridge.domain.fit_scoring scorer supplies weighting,
   hard-blocker detection, confidence, and recommendation thresholds.
6. Structured analysis is persisted as:
   ANALYSIS#<job_id>#<description_fingerprint>
7. Fit snapshots are persisted as:
   FIT#<job_id>#<profile_fingerprint>#<description_fingerprint>
8. Unchanged descriptions reuse JobAnalysis across profile changes. Unchanged
   descriptions and profiles reuse the complete fit snapshot.
9. Changed descriptions create a new analysis and fit snapshot.

The discovery store still has no method that creates a JobApplication.
