Job Discovery result-quality policy validation
==============================================

Implemented:
- Recommended: Strong/Good recommendations, minimum Job Fit 60 by default, High/Medium confidence by default.
- Possible matches: qualifying Stretch opportunities.
- Low matches: "Low match — Probably not worth your time" results separated from Recommended.
- Low-confidence and sub-threshold results hidden by default but available by broadening filters.
- Filters: minimum Job Fit, confidence, recommendation tier.
- Sorts: recommended order, Job Fit, confidence, newest posting.
- Recommended order: recommendation tier, Job Fit, confidence, Preference Fit, freshness.
- Materialized DynamoDB index version bumped and keyed by filter/sort state.
- Compact index records store recommendation tier, confidence tier, and visibility category.
