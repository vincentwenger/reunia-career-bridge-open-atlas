Job Discovery separate score validation
=======================================

Implemented behavior
--------------------
1. Job Fit remains the shared evidence-grounded score produced by
   career_bridge.domain.fit_scoring from supported, partial, and unsupported
   requirements.
2. Desired title, location, workplace type, employment type, salary, and
   posting age do not alter Job Fit.
3. Preference Fit is calculated separately from configured search preferences:
   title 30%, location 25%, workplace 20%, employment type 10%, and salary 15%
   when published.
4. Posting Freshness is calculated separately from posted_at, falling back to
   first_seen_at.
5. Search Priority is calculated transparently as:
   70% Job Fit + 20% Preference Fit + 10% Posting Freshness.
6. RankedJob exposes fit_score, preference_score, freshness_score,
   search_priority, priority_formula, and the Stage 1 preference component
   breakdown. Ranked lists sort by search_priority.
7. JobFitSnapshot persists only evidence-fit data. Search Priority is computed
   at ranking time because freshness changes over time.
8. CandidateJobProfile.evidence_fingerprint excludes search preferences, so a
   preference-only change reuses the existing fit snapshot and recomputes the
   preference and priority values locally.
9. AI-derived requirements that only describe compensation, work location,
   workplace arrangement, relocation, or employment type are removed before
   evidence scoring. Work authorization, citizenship, clearance, and required
   professional licenses remain eligible hard blockers.
