Career Translation and Resume Terminology Consolidation
=======================================================

Canonical user-facing terms
---------------------------
- Imported Resume: the original uploaded file.
- Verified Resume Evidence: structured, traceable facts extracted from the Imported Resume and confirmed evidence.
- Career Baseline Resume: the reusable target-market-language resume created in Career Translation.
- Application Baseline: the frozen starting resume for one application before tailoring.
- Target-Market Review: the application-specific review of titles, credentials, terminology, transferable experience, and evidence gaps.

Compatibility
-------------
- Internal workflow keys, routes, serialized field names, and report identifiers remain unchanged.
- Existing application records storing "Initial Resume" are displayed as "Application Baseline" through a legacy label map.

Updated surfaces
----------------
- Career Translation setup and preview.
- Resume Workflow steps, comparisons, reports, loading states, and messages.
- Interview Preparation evidence descriptions.
- Job application resume-version display.
- Analytics, marketing copy, User Guide, presentation metadata, and deployment/validation documentation.
- AI prompts and generated evidence explanations.
