UKG Pro / UltiPro Job Discovery connector

- Adds JobSourceType.UKG_PRO and the UI label "UKG Pro / UltiPro".
- Accepts public recruiting.ultipro.com, recruiting<number>.ultipro.com, and matching .ca JobBoard URLs.
- Extracts tenant and board UUID from the public URL.
- Uses bounded POST pagination through JobBoardView/LoadSearchResults.
- Enriches descriptions through public OpportunityDetail pages.
- Applies robots.txt, exact-host, redirect, timeout, response-size, rate-limit, and refresh-budget safeguards.
- Supports CSV/JSON aliases: UKG, UKG Pro, UKG Pro Recruiting, UltiPro.
- Updates Washington Trust Bank in the Oregon/Washington example CSV.
