SAP SuccessFactors Job Discovery Connector
==========================================

Implemented
-----------
- Added JobSourceType.SUCCESSFACTORS and a first-class SuccessFactorsJobSource adapter.
- Accepts public SAP-hosted and employer-owned Career Site Builder URLs.
- Canonicalizes root/brand URLs to public search URLs.
- Reads bounded search-result pagination and public job-detail pages.
- Extracts schema.org JobPosting fields with safe plain-text descriptions.
- Honors robots.txt, exact-host restrictions, response limits, redirect limits, timeouts, and per-company rate limits.
- Adds browser-safe limits and deferred detail enrichment.
- Integrates with shared public catalogs, CSV/JSON imports, source forms, posting-detail enrichment, and the common connector contract.
