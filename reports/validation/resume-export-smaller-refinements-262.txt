Career Bridge 262 - Word/PDF resume refinement validation

Implemented
- Employer names are bold rather than underlined in both Word and PDF exports.
- Mid-career corporate Word export now uses Arial so its font choice matches the PDF runtime family on Windows and the compatible Liberation Sans fallback on Linux.
- Removed Word-only expanded character spacing and small-caps styling from name, target title, contact, and section headings.
- Aligned Word line-spacing values with the PDF leading values for header, summary, skills, employer, role, bullets, and education styles.
- Applied employer-entry spacing per experience: no unnecessary gap before the first employer and a 6-point gap before later employers.
- Matched PDF bullet and education-detail indentation to Word.
- Changed the PDF education detail marker from a hyphen to the same bullet glyph used by Word.
- Widened the PDF education content column to mirror Word's right-tab layout and avoid premature wrapping or single-entry page spillover.
- Retained right-aligned dates and hyperlink underlining.

- New visual-parity unit tests verify employer emphasis, tracking removal, employer spacing, bullet indentation, line spacing, education marker, and PDF education column width.
