Oracle Cloud HCM Job Discovery Connector
========================================

Date: 2026-08-01

Implemented
-----------
- Added JobSourceType.ORACLE_CLOUD_HCM and a first-class OracleCloudHcmJobSource adapter.
- Added "Oracle Cloud HCM" to the Discovery settings source selector.
- Accepts public Oracle Candidate Experience career-site and job-detail URLs,
  including employer vanity domains that retain the Candidate Experience path.
- Canonicalizes job-detail and filtered URLs to one shared company catalog identity.
- Reads bounded public Candidate Experience requisition listings and job details.
- Falls back to embedded Oracle job data, JSON-LD, and HTML job cards when the
  requisition resource is unavailable or disabled.
- Maps job title, requisition ID, description, locations, workplace type,
  employment type, posting and closing dates, department, skills, and salary data.
- Rejects cross-host posting and apply URLs and preserves the configured career-site host.
- Honors robots.txt, exact-host redirects, response-size limits, timeouts,
  pagination/job caps, and shared per-company request spacing.
- Adds browser-safe refresh limits and deferred full-description enrichment.
- Integrates with shared public catalogs, CSV/JSON imports, source forms,
  posting-detail enrichment, and the common connector contract.

Compatibility note
------------------
Oracle documents the Candidate Experience requisition resources as internal-use.
This connector therefore treats them as a best-effort integration with the public
career site and retains embedded-data, JSON-LD, and HTML fallbacks.
