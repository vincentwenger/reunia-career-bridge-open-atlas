PeopleAdmin robots-safe indexed discovery fallback

Problem
- Portland State University disallows automated access to /postings/search.
- The previous connector correctly stopped with Permission required, but could not discover detail pages that remain public and individually permitted.

Change
- Added an OpenAI hosted web-search fallback for PeopleAdmin listing-route denials.
- Web search is restricted to the exact configured host.
- Only HTTPS /postings/<numeric-id> URLs are accepted.
- The blocked /postings/search route is never requested after denial.
- Every discovered detail URL still passes the existing robots, DNS, redirect, timeout, response-size, and rate-limit controls.
- Indexed scans are marked partial; previously collected jobs are preserved rather than counted as missing.
- Fallback can be disabled globally with JOB_DISCOVERY_INDEXED_SEARCH_FALLBACK=false or per source with indexed_search_fallback=false.

Validation
- PeopleAdmin listing denial triggers indexed URL discovery.
- Allowed detail pages are fetched and converted to jobs.
- Empty indexed results retain the Permission required error.
- Exact-host and /postings/<id> filtering rejects search pages, third-party domains, and subdomain substitutions.
