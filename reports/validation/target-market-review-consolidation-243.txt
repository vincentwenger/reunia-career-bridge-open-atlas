Career Bridge 243 — Target-Market Review consolidation

Resolved behavior

- Zero-count status chips are hidden.
- Evidence-question cards link directly to the confirmation questions.

- Requirements translation only upgrades when a concrete role or supplemental-evidence record demonstrates it; a profile skill alone remains one focused question.
