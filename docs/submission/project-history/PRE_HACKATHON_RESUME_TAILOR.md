# Pre-Hackathon Resume Tailor Summary

## Status before June 20, 2026

A rudimentary **Resume Tailor** prototype existed before the Open Atlas submission window opened on June 20, 2026.

The prototype supplied early concepts for:

- résumé import and parsing;
- job-description analysis;
- evidence-grounded résumé tailoring;
- comparing résumé versions;
- reviewing whether claims were supported by candidate evidence;
- generating résumé/document exports;
- storing basic candidate or career context.

## What this disclosure does not claim

The submission does not claim that these baseline Resume Tailor concepts were invented during Open Atlas. It also does not claim that the pre-hackathon prototype already contained the integrated Career Bridge experience.

The final Career Bridge product substantially expanded the prototype with reusable candidate-confirmed evidence, target-market career translation, per-application baselines and workflows, interview preparation and practice, action planning, job discovery, durable cloud persistence, background processing, and a unified interface.
