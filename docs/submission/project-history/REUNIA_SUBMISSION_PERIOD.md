# Réunia Submission-Period Development Summary

## Timing

The original **Réunia** project was developed as a separate AI meeting assistant during the Open Atlas submission period. Surviving development history places Réunia development underway no later than **June 22, 2026**, after the submission window opened on **June 20, 2026**.

For that reason, the Career Bridge disclosure treats Réunia as an **earlier codebase in the product lineage**, not as a pre-hackathon product.

## Foundations later adapted into Career Bridge

Réunia contributed or influenced foundations such as:

- the Flask application shell and production entry points;
- authentication and account flows;
- OpenAI integration and usage controls;
- DynamoDB/S3 repository and document-storage patterns;
- administrative settings, analytics, and support foundations;
- generic action/follow-up concepts;
- recording, transcription, and review concepts that informed later mock-interview work.

Those foundations were subsequently simplified, repurposed, or retired as the product became Career Bridge.

## Development sequence context

The earlier provenance review found Réunia artifacts from late June through July, including transcript/scorecard work, recorder and code-pipeline work, repository/testing improvements, Knowledge and Live Q&A, and a demo/judge package. These are useful evidence that Réunia itself was active submission-period development rather than a completed pre-hackathon product.

The Career Bridge integration began on **July 28, 2026**.
