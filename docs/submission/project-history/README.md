# Project History Review Guide

This folder gives reviewers a concise provenance view of **Réunia Career Bridge** while preserving the original development chronology. The published Git copy includes privacy-only sanitization of personal candidate data; commit messages, authorship, dates, and development ordering were preserved and were not backdated to manufacture activity.

## Three-stage lineage

1. **Before June 20, 2026 — Resume Tailor**
   A rudimentary Resume Tailor prototype already existed. It is the principal pre-hackathon product foundation disclosed by this submission.

2. **By June 22, 2026 — Réunia**
   Development of the separate Réunia AI meeting assistant was underway after the Open Atlas submission window had opened. Réunia is therefore a predecessor codebase, but not a pre-hackathon product.

3. **July 28, 2026 — Career Bridge integration begins**
   Selected foundations from Réunia and Resume Tailor began being integrated into the unified Réunia Career Bridge application. The Career Bridge-specific journey and the majority of the unified product functionality were built or substantially expanded during the submission period.
