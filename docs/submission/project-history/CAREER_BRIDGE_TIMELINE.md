## Important interpretation

The presence of Réunia code in the Career Bridge ancestry does **not** mean Réunia was pre-hackathon. The relevant pre-hackathon product foundation is the rudimentary Resume Tailor prototype. Réunia and the later Career Bridge integration both fall within the submission period based on the history reviewed to date.
